Arrow Keys move camera (Also, mouse to edge does the same)
S stops current selected units orders
Space returns view to base
\ Selects an idle gatherer
P allows setting a patrol from current location to target
Left click selects, or orders if A/P used
Right click otherwise gives orders for movement, rally points
Tab selects all military units across the map
CRTL+# makes a control group of selected units.
# (A integer key, chosen with the above command) selects pre-made group
